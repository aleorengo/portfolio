Le Creazioni
di Giansy

A warm, personal website for handmade biscuits, preserves and seasonal treats, with an interactive tray to build your own selection.

Visit website↗︎

Goal

Create an inviting digital home for handmade treats, making discovery and choosing a personal assortment feel special.

Target Audience

People looking for a thoughtful gift, a sweet selection to share, or a small treat for themselves.

My Role

Web design · Visual direction

Design Choices

Deep burgundy and warm cream create an intimate, welcoming atmosphere.

Script and editorial serif typography pair with clear everyday navigation.

A silver tray, decorated biscuits and a soft checked background give the experience a handmade character.

The Experience

Explore biscuits, preserves and seasonal creations.

Build a selection on the tray and adjust quantities in the accompanying summary.
