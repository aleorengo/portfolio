Web design · Barcelona city guide

¿vienes?

A colourful Barcelona city guide for local plans, neighbourhood fiestas and favourite places.

Visit website↗︎

Goal

Bring Barcelona’s plans, neighbourhood fiestas and favourite addresses into one easy-to-browse guide.

Target Audience

People living in or visiting Barcelona who want to discover local events, places and recommendations.

My Role

Web design · Visual direction

Design Choices

Expressive floral imagery and halftone textures give the guide a playful identity.

Warm paper tones and burgundy type balance personality with clear navigation.

The Experience

Browse plans, explore the city calendar and find places in the directory.

English, Spanish and Catalan navigation welcomes different audiences.

The Idea

Less searching, more living: a colourful starting point for finding your next favourite spot in Barcelona.

Visual identity

A guide
with character.

Design direction

Soft colour, oversized flowers and tactile halftone details bring the energy of the city into a clear, editorial layout.
