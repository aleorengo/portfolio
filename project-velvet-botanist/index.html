Velvet Botanist

Velvet Botanist is a fictional cocktail bar brand created as a personal project to explore an elegant, moody web experience for a hospitality concept.

Website in progress — currently being built.

Goal

Design a refined, atmospheric website that:

Communicates the bar’s botanical, craft cocktail experience

Highlights menu, events & reservations

Works smoothly on both desktop and mobile

Includes immersive visuals and inviting interactions

Target Audience

Cocktail lovers, night-life seekers, and people looking for unique, nature-inspired bar experiences.

My Role

UX/UI Design · Visual Direction · Framer Development

Defined a clear site structure (home, menu, philosophy, events, reservations, contact)

Design Choices

Rich, deep color palette designed to evoke an intimate, jazz-inspired speakeasy atmosphere with a sense of warmth and understated luxury.

Refined, elegant typography selected to reinforce a timeless, premium character.

Subtle, restrained animations and interactions that enhance immersion while preserving a calm, sophisticated experience.

Outcome

A responsive website concept designed to express mood, elegance, and brand identity through careful layout composition and restrained interactions for high-end hospitality and lifestyle brands.
