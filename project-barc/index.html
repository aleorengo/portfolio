Barc

Barc is a fictional tech product website designed as a playful experiment to explore fun animations and interactive storytelling for a dog‑communication device.

Work in progress — design and development are ongoing.

View work in progress↗︎

Goal

Design a fun, interactive website that:

Showcases animations, interactions, and micro-interactions

Engages users with playful and experimental elements

Works smoothly on both desktop and mobile

Explores creative ways to present content

Target Audience

Dog Owners ;). Users curious about interactive web experiences, playful design, and experimental digital concepts.

My Role

UX/UI Design · Visual Direction · Framer Development

Defined a dynamic site structure (home, features, interactions, about, contact)

Design Choices

Minimal color palette to showcase content

Clean typography to balance readability with playfulness

Interactive animations, transitions, and hover and scroll effects to enhance engagement

Outcome

A responsive, experimental website concept designed to showcase animation, interaction design, and creative UI exploration for portfolio purposes.
