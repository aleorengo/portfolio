The person behind the work

A designer.
A little dreamer.

I’m Alessandra, an Italian designer living in Barcelona, working across web, graphic, and interior design.

I love turning ideas into experiences: whether it’s a charming website, a brand identity that tells a story, or a space that feels alive.

After over 10 years in design, I’ve learned to balance big picture thinking with an eye for the tiniest details. I approach every project creatively, mixing function with style, and I love finding unexpected ways to make things feel memorable and personal.
